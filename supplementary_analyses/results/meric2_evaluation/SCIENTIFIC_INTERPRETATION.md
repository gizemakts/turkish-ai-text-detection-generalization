# Meric2 fixed-model exploratory stress test

Meric2 was not used to fit, tune, calibrate, select, or modify any evaluated model. The distinct Meric2-augmented CE pilot checkpoint was excluded. All results are post-hoc exploratory because that pilot outcome and earlier dataset audits were known.

Transformer evaluation is restricted to the external inference verifier's authorized seeds 42, 314, and 2026 (five folds per seed; 30 CE/SupCon cells). Seeds 137 and 3407 are not used. The five TF-IDF outer models are unchanged.

Primary metrics were calculated separately for each independently trained realization. Transformer folds were averaged within seed before three-seed summaries; folds were not treated as independent experimental replicates. TF-IDF fold variability is descriptive because its outer fits overlap in training data.

The exact-word-count matches are artificial balance blocks, not HUMAN–AI source pairs. Observation-level independence and prompt lineage cannot be verified, so no causal or source-paired inference is claimed and no nominal sample-bootstrap interval is presented as definitive.

Surface differences and score correlations are associational. This experiment cannot identify historical effects or generation-prompt effects.
