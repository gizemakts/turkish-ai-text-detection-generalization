# ABSTRACT corpus

`ai_abstracts_1000.csv` contains the 1,000 generated Turkish abstracts used in the study, with `id`, `model`, and `generated_text` columns. The corresponding human-written abstracts are not distributed because their copyright and reuse conditions do not permit their publication as part of this repository.

The study's human-written abstracts were published between 2005 and 2018. Combined-v2 incorporates 1,000 ABSTRACT pairs. See `../../DATA_ACCESS.md` and `../../EXPERIMENTS.md` for evaluation details.

The released abstracts contain no source-author bylines or names of the ten NEWSPAPER source authors. Names used as academic research subjects are retained as part of the generated text.
